Shopee Ultimate Case Challenge · Thailand 2026

Cutting failed cash-on-delivery orders for Shopee Thailand

Finalist
THB 455Myearly loss from failed deliveries
85%of failures come from COD, only 35% of orders
~THB 3.5Bprojected NPV of the plan

COD buyers fail at 10x the rate of prepaid buyers, and every failed parcel costs Shopee about THB 104 in wasted delivery and lost commission. Our team proposed a reliability score that decides who gets extra checkpoints, three targeted interventions, and a cash-flow model to prove the payback.

  • BRSA 0–100 buyer reliability score with visible reasons, appeals and a path to recover.
  • U SURE?A 5-minute confirm window for at-risk buyers before the parcel ships.
  • U FREE?Buyer-chosen 3-hour delivery slots so nobody is "not home".
  • U IN?Riders help COD buyers sign up for ShopeePay at the door.
